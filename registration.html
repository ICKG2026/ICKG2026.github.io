ICKG 2026

The 17th IEEE International Conference on Knowledge Graphs

November 12-13, 2026 | Shenyang, China

Registration

For each accepted paper, at least one author must complete a full, non-student author registration and present the paper at the conference, either on site or remotely.

Student and IEEE Life Member registration categories do not qualify as full author registration. Each full author registration covers one accepted paper only. Authors with more than one accepted paper must therefore complete a separate full author registration for each paper.

Registration is available through the conference website:
https://ickg2026.scimeeting.cn/

Hotel and visa information is also available on the registration website.
