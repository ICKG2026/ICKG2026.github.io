Airport Information

Shenyang Taoxian International Airport (SHE) is the main airport serving Shenyang. It is approximately 20 km south of downtown Shenyang. All domestic and international passenger flights operate through Terminal 3.

Direct regional flights are available from several Asian hubs, including Seoul Incheon, Busan, Jeju, and Singapore. International travellers may also connect through major Chinese aviation hubs such as Beijing, Shanghai, Guangzhou, and Chengdu.
